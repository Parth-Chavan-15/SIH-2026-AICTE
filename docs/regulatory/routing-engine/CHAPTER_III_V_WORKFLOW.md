## 1. CHAPTER III: COLLABORATION AND TWINNING PROGRAMMES

### Statutory Provisions (APH Chapter III Clauses 3.1 to 3.4 & Addendum Item 29)
1. Technical institutions and universities collaborating with foreign educational institutions for Twinning, Joint Degree, or Dual Degree programmes.
2. Foreign university must be in top 500 of QS / THE global rankings.
3. **Statutory Routing Pipeline:**
   `Application Submitted -> Document Scrutiny -> Physical EVC Inspection -> Executive Committee -> Council Decision`
4. **UGC Regulatory Boundary (`FUTURE_DEVELOPMENT`):**
   Clause 3.2(m) specifies compliance with *AICTE/UGC 2022 Regulations (Academic Collaboration between Indian and Foreign Higher Educational Institutions)*. Because external UGC 2022 regulations are not supplied, detailed UGC-dependent credit transfer and qualification equivalence checks are classified as `FUTURE_DEVELOPMENT`.

## 2. CHAPTER IV: UNIVERSITIES

### Statutory Provisions (APH Chapter IV Clauses 4.1 to 4.10 & Addendum Item 30–31)
1. **Scope:** Central Universities, State Public Universities, State Private Universities, and Institutions Deemed to be Universities.
2. **All-Programmes Mandate (`DOC-06`):** Universities seeking AICTE approval must apply for ALL technical programmes; partial approval is prohibited.
3. **Deemed Universities Mandate (`DOC-08` / `DOC-09`):** Under Supreme Court judgment in CA 17869-17870/2017, AICTE approval has been mandatory since AY 2018–19.
4. **Category A (Autonomous) vs Category B Evaluation:**
   - Under Addendum Item 31 Clause 4.7, universities meeting NIRF / NAAC thresholds are considered under Autonomous Category.
   - **UGC Regulatory Boundary (`FUTURE_DEVELOPMENT`):** Graded Autonomy categorisation derives from *UGC (Categorisation of Universities for Grant of Graded Autonomy) Regulations, 2018*, which is deferred to Future Development.

## 3. CHAPTER V: OPEN AND DISTANCE LEARNING (ODL) & ONLINE LEARNING (OL)

### Statutory Provisions (APH Chapter V & Addendum Item 32)
1. Standalone Institutions and Deemed to be Universities offering ODL or Online programmes in Management and Computer Applications.
2. **One Batch Pass Out Requirement:** Addendum Item 32 modifies Clause 5.3.8 to require at least **one batch pass out** for applying for ODL/OL.
3. **Mandatory Evaluation Mode:** Addendum Item 32 Note explicitly specifies: **"All the Proposals will be evaluated with SC & EVC."** Both Scrutiny Committee and Expert Visit Committee are mandatory.
4. **Bi-Annual Processing:** APH Table 2.3 specifies applications will be processed twice a year as per UGC norms.
5. **UGC Regulatory Boundary (`FUTURE_DEVELOPMENT`):** Detailed e-content, proctoring, and LMS compliance checks under *UGC (ODL and Online Programmes) Regulations, 2020* are deferred to Future Development.
